# Brand and social assets

- `public/favicon.svg`: authored geometric V on a cobalt square. Raster favicons, the ICO and Apple touch icon reproduce that geometry using Pillow.
- `public/social-ru.png` and `public/social-en.png`: authored 1200×630 PNG compositions using Pillow. They use the site's concrete, ink and cobalt palette. Headline, author name and role match `i18n/locales/ru.json` and `en.json`.
- The social artwork uses Golos Text at weight 800 from the [official Google Fonts font file](https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/golostext/v7/q5uXsoe9Lv5t7Meb31EcOR9UdVTNs822plXRQg5c.ttf).
- The site self-hosts Cyrillic and Latin variable Golos Text WOFF2 files in `app/assets/fonts`, with the SIL Open Font License in `OFL.txt`.

These are original brand compositions. They do not use stock photography, generated imagery or third-party logos.
